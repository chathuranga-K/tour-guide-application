Why YourGuide.lk

Sri Lanka has great guides. Finding a trusted one shouldn't be a gamble.

Can't tell who's licensed

Licensed and unlicensed guides look the same online, and tourists have no easy way to check.

No single place to book

Travellers rely on word of mouth and manual chats, while good guides struggle to show their credentials.

Planning is hard for newcomers

First-time visitors don't know which places to see or how to put a route together.

Features

Everything you need for a safe, smooth trip

One connected platform for tourists, guides, agencies and admins.

SLTDA verified blue tick

Guides and agencies submit licence details, our admins verify them, and the badge is removed automatically when a licence expires.

Customised tours

Choose your destinations, pick-up and drop-off points and timing. The route and fare are shown before you book.

Smart Guide Matching

Tell us your language, interests, budget and trip length, and we match you with verified guides who fit your journey.

Live location sharing

Send a trip link by SMS or email, or copy it. Your contact can follow you, and sees your last known location if your signal drops.

AI trip suggestions

New to Sri Lanka? Get destination and guide ideas with pictures and descriptions, plus a chatbot for quick answers.

Secure online payments

Pay through a trusted payment gateway with cards and wallets. We never store your card details.

Reviews & Ratings

Only tourists with a completed booking can leave a review, so ratings come from real trips.

Report an issue

Flag a suspicious profile or a problem on a trip. Reports go to our admins and the agency is alerted.

Vehicle hire

Need a ride? Set your pick-up and drop-off, choose a vehicle type, see the fare upfront and get a driver assigned, just like a ride-hailing app.

Safety first

Peace of mind for you and your family

Travelling somewhere new is easier when the people you trust can follow along. Location sharing is always opt-in, limited to your trip and under your control.

  • Share a live trip link by SMS, email or copy-and-paste
  • Last known location kept if your phone loses signal
  • Sharing ends when your trip ends or when you stop it
  • In-app reporting for guides, agencies and incidents
